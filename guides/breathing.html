breathe

three ways to change your body’s mind in under two minutes.

You don’t have to believe in anything for this to work. Breathing out for longer than you breathe in tells your nervous system the danger has passed. That’s plumbing, not faith.

01

when to use it

Reach for these before the thing, not only after it.

  • before a hard conversation
  • when your chest goes tight
  • when you can’t get to sleep
  • right after something startles you
  • when you notice you’ve been holding your breath

02

the square

box breathing · about one minute

  1. In through your nose — count 4.
  2. Hold — count 4.
  3. Out through your mouth — count 4.
  4. Hold empty — count 4.
  5. Round the square four times.

If four feels like too long, use three. Evenness matters more than the number.

03

the long way out

the long exhale · one minute

  1. In through your nose — count 4.
  2. Out, slowly, through pursed lips — count 6, or 8 if you can.
  3. Keep going for a minute.

This is the one that actually does the calming. If you remember nothing else from this page: make the out-breath longer than the in-breath.

04

the double sigh

fastest one · about ten seconds

  1. Normal breath in through your nose.
  2. On top of it, sip in a second, smaller breath.
  3. Let it all go, slowly, out of your mouth.
  4. Twice is usually enough.

Good for the ten seconds before you cry, or shout, or send the message you shouldn’t send.
