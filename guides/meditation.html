a free workbook

sit

five minutes. no cushion, no incense, no clearing your mind.

Meditation is not emptying your head. It’s noticing where your head went, and coming back. The coming back is the practice. Which means you cannot be bad at this — a wandering mind is the exercise equipment, not the failure.

01

set it up

  • pick a time you’re already still — before sleep, after coffee
  • set a timer for five minutes
  • sit however is comfortable, feet on the floor
  • eyes closed, or resting soft on one spot

02

the five minutes

do this

  1. Notice three things you can hear.
  2. Find your breath. Don’t change it — just find it.
  3. When you catch yourself thinking, say the word thinking in your head.
  4. Come back to the breath.
  5. Repeat until the timer goes.

03

when your mind wanders

It will. Twenty times in five minutes is normal. That is not a sign it isn’t working.

  • it wandered — that isn’t failing
  • you noticed — that’s the rep
  • name it, come back
  • don’t grade yourself on the way

04

a week of it

One word for each day. That’s the whole tracker. mon / tue / wed / thu / fri / sat / sun

05

check

Where does your mind go most often?
Is that thing asking for your attention somewhere else in your life?
