new job nerves

for the week before you start, or the first week in.

Nerves before a new job are not a warning. They’re what caring about something feels like before you have any evidence yet. This sheet gets you the evidence.

01

what’s the fear, exactly

What are you actually afraid will happen? “it’ll go badly” is too vague to argue with. be specific.
  • I’ll look stupid
  • I won’t be able to do the work
  • they’ll realise they hired the wrong person
  • I won’t fit in with them
  • I’ll be overwhelmed and show it
  • I’ll fail where everyone can see

03

the first week is supposed to be hard

Nobody expects you to know things you haven’t been told yet. Being new is a job description, not a performance.
